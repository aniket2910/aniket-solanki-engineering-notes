# 01 — Pixar: The Story Spine & Pixar's Principles

Pixar spent decades quietly becoming the most reliable story factory on earth — not by magic, but by
turning storytelling into a *repeatable craft discipline* with checkable principles. This chapter
distills two things they made famous: the **Story Spine** (a fill-in-the-blank skeleton for any
story) and the set of **storytelling rules of thumb** that circulated out of Pixar's story room.

| ID | Lesson | Key ideas |
|----|--------|-----------|
| 001 | [The Story Spine & Pixar's Principles](001-the-story-spine-and-pixar-principles.md) | The "Once upon a time / Every day / Until one day / Because of that…" spine; character wants over needs; earning the ending; honesty and emotional truth; the rule of the appealing failure |

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Be able to draft the skeleton of *any* story in sixty seconds using the spine, and hold your draft
up against Pixar's principles to find where it's lying, cheating, or coasting.
