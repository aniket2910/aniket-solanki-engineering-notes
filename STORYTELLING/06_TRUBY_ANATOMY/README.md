# 06 — The Anatomy of Story: John Truby

If Snyder's beat sheet is a template you *pour a story into*, John Truby's *The Anatomy of Story*
(2007) is the direct rebellion against that. Truby argues great stories aren't built from external
templates — they **grow organically** from a single seed (the premise) like a plant from a bud, and
the writer's real job is a **moral argument** dramatized through a **web of characters.** This is the
deepest, most character-and-meaning-focused system in the course.

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Learn to grow a story from the inside out — from a character's moral weakness and need — instead of
stamping it onto a template. Master the 7-step spine, the character web, and the idea that plot,
character, and theme are the *same thing* seen from three angles.
