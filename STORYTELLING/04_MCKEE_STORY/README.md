# 04 — Story: Robert McKee

Campbell gives you the mythic *shape*. Robert McKee gives you the *physics* — the deep principles that
make any scene, in any genre, actually generate feeling. His book *Story* (1997), drawn from his
legendary decades-long "Story Seminar," is treated as the screenwriter's bible. Its most valuable
idea is almost mechanical: story runs on the **gap between what a character expects and what actually
happens**, and every scene must **turn on a change of value.**

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Stop thinking of a story as "events" and start seeing it as a **chain of value-changes driven by the
gap between expectation and reality.** Be able to look at any scene and ask the two McKee questions:
*what value is at stake, and how does it flip?*
