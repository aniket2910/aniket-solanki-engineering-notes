# 08 — Made to Stick: Chip & Dan Heath

The earlier books are about *stories*. This one is about *ideas* — and why some ideas (urban legends,
proverbs, conspiracy theories) lodge in memory effortlessly while important, true ideas evaporate. The
Heath brothers' *Made to Stick* (2007) reverse-engineers "stickiness" into six principles (the acronym
**SUCCESs**), and reveals that **story is the delivery vehicle that makes the other five stick.** This
is storytelling aimed at communication, teaching, pitching, and persuasion — the most directly useful
chapter for your day job.

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Diagnose *why* your explanations, pitches, and docs fail to land, name the specific fix (which of the
six principles you're missing), and understand why wrapping an idea in a story is the single highest-
leverage move for making it memorable and actionable.
