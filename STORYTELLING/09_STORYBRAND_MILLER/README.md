# 09 — Building a StoryBrand: Donald Miller

This is the Hero's Journey pointed straight at business. Donald Miller's *Building a StoryBrand* (2017)
makes one deceptively simple, high-consequence claim: in your brand's story, **the customer is the
hero — not you.** Your company is the *guide* (Yoda, Gandalf, the mentor), and the moment you cast
yourself as the hero, you lose. The **SB7 framework** turns Campbell's monomyth into a 7-part template
for websites, pitches, and marketing that actually converts.

| ID | Lesson | Key ideas |
|----|--------|-----------|
| 001 | [The Customer Is the Hero — the SB7 Framework](001-customer-is-the-hero-sb7.md) | Position the customer as hero and the brand as guide; the 7 parts (character, problem, guide, plan, call to action, failure, success); external/internal/philosophical problems; the grunt test; identity transformation |

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Learn to describe any product, service, or even yourself so the *audience* is the hero of the story and
you are the guide who helps them win — the single positioning shift that clarifies most marketing,
pitching, and personal-brand messaging.
