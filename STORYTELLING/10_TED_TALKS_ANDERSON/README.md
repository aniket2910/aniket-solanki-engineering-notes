# 10 — TED Talks: Chris Anderson

The course ends where storytelling meets you most directly: standing up and speaking. Chris Anderson —
the man who runs TED — wrote *TED Talks: The Official TED Guide to Public Speaking* (2016) after
watching thousands of talks succeed and fail from the front row. His central reframe: a talk is not a
performance and not information-transfer — it's **idea-building**, the act of reconstructing an idea
inside the audience's mind. And the tool that makes it cohere is the **through-line.**

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Be able to construct a talk (or any spoken pitch) around a single through-line, start from what the
audience already knows, and use narrative to *build* your idea in their heads rather than dumping
information at them.
