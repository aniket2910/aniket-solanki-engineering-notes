# 07 — Wired for Story: Lisa Cron

Everything so far describes *how* to build a story. Lisa Cron's *Wired for Story* (2012) asks the
deeper question: **why does the human brain crave story at all?** Her answer, drawn from cognitive
science, is that story isn't entertainment layered on top of survival — it *is* a survival tool: a
simulator the brain evolved to rehearse life before living it. Understanding that rewires how you
write, because it tells you what the brain is actually hunting for on every line.

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Understand the cognitive *why* beneath every technique in this course, and turn it into a practical
lens: on every sentence, know what the reader's brain is actually asking, and make sure you're
answering it.
