# 05 — Save the Cat!: Blake Snyder

McKee gives you the physics; Blake Snyder gives you the **blueprint** — a literal 15-beat checklist,
with page numbers, that tells you *what should happen and roughly when.* *Save the Cat!* (2005) is the
most practical, most-used, and most-argued-about plotting tool in modern screenwriting. It's the one
that makes people say "all movies feel the same now" — which is exactly why you should understand it:
to use its power and avoid its trap.

## 🎯 Goal of this chapter

Be able to lay out the skeleton of a satisfying feature-length story on 15 beats, know the *function*
of each beat (not just its name), and understand why the beat sheet is a powerful scaffold but a
dangerous master.
